## Acceptance criteria
Accurate recognizable silhouettes; coherent palettes; editable color regions; no accidental fill overlaps; clean small-size details; preview at intended physical size; test stitch generation, coverage and density, hoop bounds, trims and color sequence. Distinguish draft from validated assets. Record provenance and redistribution permission for third-party material. Provide individual motifs and full compositions, with optional native editable lettering rather than flattened placeholder text. Review actual sew-outs before labeling production-ready.
